## F. F-1 "Duration of Status" FINAL RULE — published Jul 17 2026, effective **Sep 15 2026**
- Sources: Yale OISS summary (Jul 22 2026) https://oiss.yale.edu/immigration/elimination-of-duration-of-status-summary-faqs ; DHS Study in the States quick facts; Forbes Jul 17 2026; Federal Register proposed rule Aug 28 2025 (2025-16554).
- Directly affects a Fall 2027 international entrant:
  1. Admission is for the I-20 program end date **or 4 years, whichever is shorter** (+30-day grace) — a 4-yr BS Aug 2027–May 2031 gets an I-94 to ~Jun 18 2031. Any extra semester, change of level (BS→MS), or **post-completion OPT requires a USCIS Extension of Stay (Form I-539) or travel-and-reentry**. OPT/STEM-OPT *eligibility* unchanged, but the process now has a USCIS adjudication step.
  2. **Grace period cut 60 → 30 days** after program end.
  3. **First-year undergrads may not change major, transfer, or change level** (absent SEVP exception). After year 1 they may. → International students should apply directly to the intended major (CS vs CE) rather than plan to switch in year 1. (Implementation of the mobility restrictions may be delayed to Sep 14 2028 at DHS discretion.)
  4. Graduate students may not change program/major/transfer at any point during the program.
  5. No lateral/downward progression (no second bachelor's/second master's in F-1).
  6. Subject to Congressional Review Act; litigation likely but none noted as of Jul 22.
- Guide §9.5 mentions "proposals to fee OPT" and Day-1 CPT; it does NOT cover the D/S rule → add a "What changed in 2026" block to §9.5 with the 5 practical implications above. This is the single most material update for the international-student reader.
