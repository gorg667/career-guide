Which career fits me?

Ten questions, two minutes. This turns the decision matrix in Section 10.2 and the personality notes in Section 2.5 into a ranked shortlist. It is a starting point for reading, not an oracle: the guide's own advice is that any Tier 1 field pursued with depth beats any Tier 2 field pursued generically, and that fit — what you will actually go deep in — matters more than rank. Nothing you enter leaves your browser.

How the scoring works. Each answer adds points to careers with matching traits (e.g., "I want to see my work in physical objects" boosts hardware-adjacent careers) and adjusts how much weight the six dimensions get (e.g., "pay matters most" raises D4). The final score is 60% trait match, 40% weighted dimension total. Hard constraints — not a US citizen, not willing to do grad school — remove careers rather than penalize them. Ties and near-ties are common; read all three top profiles.
