Recruiting windows (Gantt view)

Bars show when applications are typically open. The single most expensive mistake in the guide is missing the August–October opening of each cycle — many programs fill on a rolling basis long before their stated deadline.

Period by period

    Dates are typical, not guaranteed. Big Tech, quant, and defense primes open in August–September; smaller companies recruit January–April; REU deadlines cluster in February; PhD applications are due December 1–15; MS applications December–March.
