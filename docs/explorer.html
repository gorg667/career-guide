Every career profiled in the guide, scored on the six dimensions from Section 2.1. The default total is the plain sum used in Section 10.1. Drag the weight sliders to re-rank by what matters to you — turn compensation down and moat up if you want a low-competition, stable path; turn optionality up if you are undecided. Click a column header to sort; click a career name to open its profile.
